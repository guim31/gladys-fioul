## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- The weekly national average price of heating oil in France (deliveries of
  2,000 to 4,999 litres, taxes included), from the ministry of ecological
  transition (DGEC): price, change over one week, average of the previous
  month, week of the survey.
- The estimated cost of a fill, with the litres you usually order.
- Optionally, the national averages of the road fuels of the same file
  (diesel, SP95-E10, SP95-E5, SP98, E85, LPG, off-road diesel) on a second
  device.
- Scene trigger "New heating oil price", filtered by trend, with a
  ready-made summary.
- Dashboard widget: price, trend, cost of a fill, curve of the last six
  months, previous month's average.
- "Test the download" action.
- Documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
